Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 · SnapStore for Android

1. Introduction

Welcome to SnapStore ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by Ade Fathudin. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our transparent data practices for the Android application SnapStore - Play Store Screenshot Maker (Package ID: com.adefathudin.snapstore).

Our architectural philosophy is simple: your screenshots and creative assets belong exclusively to you. SnapStore is built as an offline-first tool that performs all image processing, gradient rendering, device framing, and typography composition locally on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

SnapStore does not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal credentials (such as email, name, phone number, or social logins) to access all features.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Your raw screenshots, gallery images, custom text, and exported mockup renders are processed 100% within your Android device's local memory and storage. They are never transmitted to our servers or any cloud platform.
  • No Tracking or Analytics SDKs: We do not embed third-party surveillance trackers, advertising profiling SDKs, or behavioral analytics tools into the app.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

SnapStore requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its core screenshot generation functionality:

  • Read Media Images / Gallery Permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used strictly to let you select raw app screenshots from your photo gallery to insert into smartphone mockup frames.
  • Write / Save Images (Scoped Storage): Used to save your finished, high-resolution mockup graphics directly into your device's Pictures/SnapStore or standard public photo directory so you can upload them to the Google Play Console.

You maintain full control to grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device's System Settings.

4. Google Play Services & In-App Updates

SnapStore integrates the official Google Play In-App Update API (provided by Google Play Core). This feature checks if an updated version of SnapStore is available in the Google Play Store and provides a standard system dialog to update smoothly.

This interaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. SnapStore does not capture, store, or monitor your Google account credentials during this update process.

5. Local Data Storage & Persistence

Your editor preferences (such as selected color presets, custom hex codes, typography sizes, and active slide drafts) are stored locally in your device's private app sandbox storage (via Android Jetpack DataStore / Room).

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the application or clear application data via system settings, this local preference data is immediately erased.

6. Children's Privacy

SnapStore is a professional utility tool suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since no personal data is gathered from any user, SnapStore fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect potential new features or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date".

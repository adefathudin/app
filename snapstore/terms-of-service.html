3. Scope & Nature of the Application

SnapStore is a standalone offline-first mobile creative utility that enables developers, marketers, and creators to:

  • Import raw mobile app screenshots from the local photo gallery into device mockup frames.
  • Customize layout dimensions, background gradients, typography, and headline styles.
  • Organize multiple screenshot slides into an ordered carousel (P1, P2, P3, etc.).
  • Export high-resolution PNG image files calibrated for Google Play Store Console listings.

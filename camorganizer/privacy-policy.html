Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CamOrganizer ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information when you use our app.

2. Data Collection and Storage

CamOrganizer does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, photos, or videos to external servers.

All photos, videos, watermarks, and folder configurations created using CamOrganizer remain 100% local to your device. We do not have access to your media files, camera feed, or personal information.

3. Permissions Requested

To provide its core camera and folder organization functionality, CamOrganizer requests the following permissions on your device:

  • CAMERA: Required to capture photos and record videos inside the app.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required to capture sound during video recording.
  • STORAGE / SAF (Storage Access Framework): Required to create folders and save photos/videos directly into your chosen directories.
  • INTERNET: Required to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CamOrganizer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can learn more about how Google handles ad data by visiting Google’s Advertising Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

CamOrganizer is safe for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

1. Introduction

Welcome to CamOrganizer (package: com.adefathudin.camorganizer), developed by Ade Fathudin ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles data and permissions when you capture, organize, and manage media.

🔒

2. Data Collection and Storage

Key Principle: CamOrganizer does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your photos, videos, or camera feed on any external server.

Photos, videos, folder configurations, and tags created through CamOrganizer are saved locally on your Android device in standard folders (such as DCIM or locations chosen via the Android Storage Access Framework).

Because your media is stored purely on your hardware, we have no access, ownership, or ability to view your captured content.

🛡️

3. Permissions Requested

CamOrganizer requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to perform its core camera and file organization features:

Permission Classification Purpose & Usage in CamOrganizer
CAMERA Core Required to preview camera feed, snap photos, and record video clips.
RECORD_AUDIO Core Required to capture audio sound when you record video.
Storage / SAF Core Allows the app to write captured files directly into your selected custom folders.
INTERNET Supporting Used exclusively to fetch and display banner/interstitial ads via Google AdMob.

We do not request sensitive background location permissions, contact list access, phone call logs, or SMS data.

📢

4. Advertising via Google AdMob

CamOrganizer uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements that support ongoing development and maintenance of the application.

Google and its certified advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), coarse network info (IP address), and generic app telemetry in accordance with Google's privacy guidelines.

5. Children's Privacy

CamOrganizer is designed for a general audience and does not intentionally collect personal identification information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and address the matter promptly.

Any revisions will be posted directly to this web page with a refreshed "Effective Date." We recommend reviewing this document occasionally to stay informed.

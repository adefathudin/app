Privacy Policy

We believe your photos and videos should stay under your control. This policy explains how CamOrganizer handles your information and device permissions.

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

👋

1. Introduction

Welcome to CamOrganizer ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles information when you use CamOrganizer.

🔒

2. Data Collection and Storage

CamOrganizer does not collect, store, or transmit your photos or videos to our own external servers.

Photos, videos, folder configurations, and camera-related content created through CamOrganizer are stored locally on your device or in the storage location selected by you.

We do not have direct access to your private media files or camera feed.

🛡️

3. Permissions Requested

CamOrganizer requests certain Android permissions to provide its core camera and media organization functionality.

  • CAMERA — Required to capture photos and record videos inside the app.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — Required to capture sound during video recording.
  • Storage / Storage Access Framework — Used to save photos and videos and manage folders in locations selected by the user.
  • INTERNET — Used for services such as displaying advertisements through Google AdMob.
📢

4. Advertising

CamOrganizer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, and advertising-related usage information in accordance with their applicable policies.

For more information about how Google handles information for advertising, please visit:

Google Advertising Privacy & Terms →

👨‍👩‍👧

5. Children's Privacy

CamOrganizer is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

🔄

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

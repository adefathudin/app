3. Scope & Nature of the Application

CamOrganizer is a local-first Android camera utility application designed to help users capture and categorize multimedia content efficiently. Core features include:

  • Direct Folder Routing: Capturing photos and recording video clips directly into pre-selected or newly created custom device folders (such as DCIM/CamOrganizer or custom directories selected via Android Storage Access Framework).
  • In-Camera Workflow: Custom folder selectors, session shortcuts, grid overlays, and camera settings adjustments.
  • 100% Local-First Architecture: All captured photos and videos are written directly to your device storage without being transmitted to, analyzed by, or stored on any external server.
  • Optional Pro / Premium Upgrades: Optional enhancements, such as ad removal or advanced folder rules, made available via in-app purchases.
